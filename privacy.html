Privacy

Tandy Messenger · last updated 2026-10-23

This site doesn't collect personal information. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking. There are no forms.

What happens when you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and keep the service secure; see the GitHub privacy statement. All fonts, images and code come from this site itself, so no other company receives your visit.

Links

Links to other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

Your rights and contact

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and similar laws, you can ask what information we hold about you and ask to correct or delete it. This site holds no information about its visitors, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete.
