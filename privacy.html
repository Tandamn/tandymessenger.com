אחסון ומידע טכני

GitHub מתארת את העיבוד, משך השמירה, נותני השירות וההעברות לחו"ל בהצהרת הפרטיות שלה. מידע כזה עשוי להיות מעובד מחוץ לישראל, כולל בארצות הברית.

Privacy

tandamn.com and tandymessenger.com · last updated 2026-10-23

Scope

This notice covers the tandamn.com and tandymessenger.com websites. The Tandy Messenger app has its own privacy notice and account deletion page.

What our website code collects

These are static marketing sites. Their code has no forms, analytics or advertising trackers, sets no cookies, and stores nothing in your browser. Display settings on the accessible version last only while the page is open.

Hosting and technical data

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, GitHub and service providers it uses may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as your IP address, browser and device details, the address you requested, the page you came from, and the time of the request. GitHub describes its processing, retention, service providers and international transfers in its privacy statement. This information may be processed outside Israel, including in the United States.

External links

If you follow a link to another service, that service processes information under its own terms and privacy notice.

Retention

We do not run a visitor database through the website code. Technical information processed by GitHub and its service providers is kept according to their policies. If we start collecting more ourselves, we will update this notice first.

Your rights and contact

Under Israel's Privacy Protection Law, 1981, and similar laws such as the GDPR, you may have rights regarding personal information, such as access, correction and deletion. For privacy requests write to tandamn.app@gmail.com. Requests about information GitHub controls independently may need to go to GitHub.
