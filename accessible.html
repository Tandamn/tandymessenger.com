Tandy Messenger: group chats that build things

The AI group chat app.

Tandy is the AI llama in your group chat. Ask him for a checklist, a plan, a vote or a game, and it pops up for everyone, ready to use.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How it works

  1. Chat like always.

    Your friends, your plans, your inside jokes.

  2. Ask Tandy. Just you two.

    Your group sees it only when you share it.

  3. Share it. Play together.

    Everyone taps, votes and plays right in the chat.

For example: Maya asks the group "Friday night, who's in?". Without the group seeing, you ask Tandy to make a vote for Friday, he builds one with Pizza night, Bowling and Movie marathon, you share it, and everyone votes in the chat. Pizza wins.

Ask for anything

Things friends and families make with Tandy, for getting things done and for fun. Or describe something new and watch it appear.

  • Checklists: packing, groceries, to-dos.
  • Trip plans: the trip, day by day.
  • Split the bill: who owes who, solved.
  • Who's in?: RSVPs without the chaos.
  • Countdowns: 12 days to the trip.
  • Quizzes: how well do you know us.
  • Spin the wheel: let fate pick the movie.
  • Truth or dare: game night, ready.
  • Your idea here: describe it, Tandy builds it.

Questions, answered

What is Tandy Messenger?

Tandy Messenger is a group chat app with Tandy, an AI llama who makes checklists, plans, polls, quizzes, games and other little apps for your group, right inside the chat.

When is Tandy Messenger coming out?

Soon. Tandy Messenger is on its way to the App Store and Google Play.

Will Tandy Messenger be on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Tandy Messenger is coming to both iPhone and Android.

Who sees what I ask Tandy?

Not your group. Your request and what Tandy makes appear in the group only when you choose to share them. Tandy answers using Claude, Anthropic's AI service, and only after you agree to it; the app's privacy notice explains what is sent.

Who makes Tandy Messenger?

Tandy Messenger is made by Tandamn!, a small company that makes things worth a damn.
